Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · SlimeFi Telegram Mini App · Bot: @slimefi_bot · Channel: @SlimeFi_official

1. What SlimeFi is

SlimeFi is a Telegram Mini App that opens inside Telegram — no separate download or sign-up is needed. You collect SlimeFi Coins by watching ads, completing tasks, playing games, claiming a daily bonus and inviting friends, and you can redeem coins for real money to your UPI account once you reach ₹10 (1,000 coins = ₹1).

2. What data we collect

  • Telegram identity — your Telegram user ID, username and first name, provided by Telegram when you open the app. This is how your coin balance and progress are recognised.
  • App activity — coins earned and spent, tasks completed, games played, spins, daily-bonus streaks, referrals and support tickets. Every coin movement is recorded in a ledger so payouts can be checked.
  • UPI details, only when you withdraw — the UPI ID/handle you enter for a withdrawal. We use it only to send your payout.
  • Device / ad data (via our ad partner, see §3) — advertising identifiers, IP address and ad-interaction data collected by the ad provider to serve and measure ads and detect fraud.

We never ask for, and never collect, your UPI PIN, OTP, bank password or any crypto private key. Anyone asking for these in SlimeFi's name is not us — report it through in-app Support.

3. Advertising — third-party ads (Monetag)

SlimeFi shows rewarded ads through Monetag. When you tap Watch Ad, Monetag and its advertisers may collect device and advertising data and may place cookies or similar trackers to serve, measure and verify the ad and to prevent fraud. Coins are credited only when Monetag confirms a paid (valued) ad — simply opening an ad does not earn coins. We do not control which ad creative is shown; ad content belongs to the advertiser.

4. How your data is used

  • To run the app: keep your balance, progress, streaks and leaderboard.
  • To credit rewards and process UPI withdrawals, including owner review of payouts.
  • To prevent abuse: detecting fake, duplicate or automated accounts and protecting the referral system.
  • To answer support tickets you submit in the app.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

5. How long data is kept

Coin and payout records are kept for as long as needed to audit withdrawals and resolve disputes. Support tickets are kept until resolved. If you stop using SlimeFi, your Telegram identity data remains only as part of those past records.

6. Your choices

You can stop using SlimeFi at any time by closing the Mini App or blocking the bot in Telegram. To ask about your data, correct a UPI detail, or raise a complaint, use the in-app Support & Grievance section — every request gets a ticket ID. We will never ask you for a UPI PIN, OTP or private key in reply.

7. Terms of Use (short version)

  • Coins are in-app points. They have no cash value outside SlimeFi except redemption to UPI at the rate shown in the app (today: 1,000 coins = ₹1, minimum withdrawal ₹10).
  • No earnings are guaranteed. Rewards depend on your activity and on ad availability. Any rate shown in the app can change; the rate in the app at the time applies.
  • Referral coins (today: 1,000) are paid only when the invited friend completes their first qualifying activity.
  • Withdrawals are reviewed by the app owner and can be rejected if the activity looks fake or abusive; a rejected payout is refunded to your coin balance.
  • Fake, duplicate or automated accounts, and any attempt to forge ad rewards, can lose their coins and access without payout.
  • The Slime Rocket game is for fun only — it uses demo coins and gives no reward or real money.

8. Changes to this page

If this policy or the terms change, the new version will be shown in the app and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continuing to use SlimeFi after a change means you accept the new version.

Questions? Open SlimeFi → Support & Grievance in the app, or message @slimefi_bot.
